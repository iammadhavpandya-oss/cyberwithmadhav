Who this workshop is for

This page is for college faculty, student councils, NSS units and department heads who want a cyber security awareness session their students will actually sit through. It is also useful for coaching batches that want the same topics before internships or a first job.

What a typical college session covers

I start from scams that college students in India actually face, not generic slides copied from somewhere else. A usual 60 to 90 minute session includes:

  • UPI fraud: collect requests, fake refunds and QR codes that should not be trusted blindly.
  • Fake job and internship offers, including roles that ask for a registration fee or your OTP.
  • Phishing on email, SMS and WhatsApp, and how account takeover usually starts.
  • Campus and public Wi-Fi habits, two-factor authentication, and stronger passwords.
  • Digital arrest awareness, and a clear first-hour plan: when to call 1930 and how to report at cybercrime.gov.in.

If your department wants a longer workshop, we can add a live quiz and a hands-on demo that runs only on my own test devices. There is no hacking of real accounts or real people.

Online for any college in India, offline in Mumbai

If your campus is outside Mumbai, book an online session. We agree the platform over email, for example Google Meet or Zoom. You need a stable internet link and a way for the group to see and hear the session. If your campus is in Mumbai, I can come to the venue. Please share the room setup and facilities such as a projector, a microphone and internet.

Questions colleges usually ask

What does a cyber security workshop for college students cover?

The usual starting point is UPI fraud, fake job or internship offers, phishing, account takeover, public or campus Wi-Fi, two-factor authentication, and digital arrest awareness. Tell me what your students are worried about and I will shape the session around that.

Can you give a guest lecture on cyber security at our college?

Yes. Faculty, student councils and NSS units can email to book a guest lecture or a longer workshop. Online sessions are open to colleges anywhere in India. In-person sessions are for Mumbai campuses only. Please write at least 2 weeks ahead where possible.

How long is a typical college session, and what does it cost?

Most college sessions run 60 to 90 minutes. An extended workshop can go longer if you want a quiz or a hands-on demo on my own test devices. The fee is shared on request, based on audience size and format. I reply with a clear fee before anything is booked.

Is this a placement workshop or legal advice?

No. This is an awareness and education session only. It is not a placement or employability workshop, and it is not legal advice. If someone has lost money, they should call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
