Scams and fraud help

What should I do if I lose money to a UPI scam?

Call 1930 as soon as you notice the fraud, then report on cybercrime.gov.in and tell your bank through its official helpline. You only enter your UPI PIN to send money, never to receive it, and you never need to scan a QR code to get paid. The faster you report, the better the chance that the money can be put on hold.

What should I do in the first hour after cyber fraud?

In the first 10 minutes, stop paying and call 1930. Next, call your bank's official number and block cards, UPI or net banking, then save evidence such as screenshots, transaction IDs, numbers and links. Before the hour is over, complete the complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and secure your email and phone. Speed gives the best chance that money can be put on hold.

How do I report cyber fraud in India, and what is 1930?

1930 is the National Cyber Crime Helpline. Call it as soon as you notice the fraud and you will get an acknowledgement number by SMS. Then log in at cybercrime.gov.in, complete the full complaint with that number, and tell your bank through its official helpline.

First call 1930 so you have an acknowledgement number, then open cybercrime.gov.in and log in to complete the full complaint with that number. Keep the complaint number safe, attach the evidence you saved, and also tell your bank. Reporting quickly, ideally within hours, improves the chance of freezing money in transit.

What is a digital arrest scam?

Digital arrest is a scam, not a legal procedure. The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) says there is no concept of digital arrest under any Indian law, and real police, CBI, ED or RBI officials do not arrest anyone on a video call or ask for money to settle a case. Hang up, tell a family member, verify with your nearest police station on a number you look up yourself, and if you have paid, call 1930 at once.

Why should I never share an OTP?

An OTP approves an action, like a payment, a login, a SIM change or a new device. Read the full OTP message: it usually says what it is for. If anyone asks you to read out or forward an OTP, it is a scam, no matter how official they sound. If you shared one, call your bank and 1930 right away.

How do I spot a fake job offer?

Real employers do not charge you to get a job. Offers that come without an interview, ask for a registration, training or security fee, or promise easy money for liking videos, rating hotels or completing tasks are scams. Verify every recruiter on the company's official careers page. If you paid, stop paying and call 1930.

What should I do if a loan app harasses me?

Before installing a loan app, check that it is linked to an RBI regulated bank or NBFC and appears in the RBI's public directory of Digital Lending Apps. Never give a loan app access to your contacts or gallery. If an app harasses you, do not pay extortion demands: save evidence, report on cybercrime.gov.in, inform the police, and complain to RBI if a regulated lender is involved.

How can someone take over my WhatsApp, and how do I stop it?

Most WhatsApp takeovers happen when someone tricks you into sharing the six digit registration code sent by SMS, or gets your account opened on a linked device. Turn on two step verification (a PIN that WhatsApp asks for when your number is registered on a new phone), add an email for recovery, and regularly check Linked devices. Never share the code, even with a friend.

How do I spot a phishing SMS in India?

A phishing SMS creates urgency, such as a power cut tonight or an account blocked today, and pushes you to click a link, call a number or share an OTP. Real banks, electricity boards and couriers do not ask for your PIN, OTP or card details by SMS. Do not click. Open the official app or website yourself, and report the message on Sanchar Saathi Chakshu.

What are simple safe UPI habits?

Enter your UPI PIN only to send money, never to receive it. Read the payee name before you pay, never install screen sharing apps on a caller's request, keep your phone locked and updated, and turn on transaction alerts. If money is lost, call 1930 and your bank immediately.

What is Sanchar Saathi and when should I use it?

Sanchar Saathi, from the Department of Telecommunications, lets you report suspected fraud calls and messages (Chakshu), see mobile connections in your name, block a lost or stolen phone across all networks, check a phone's IMEI before buying, and verify bank customer care contacts. It is available at sancharsaathi.gov.in and as an app. If you already lost money, use 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in instead.

How do I stay safe from a fake mParivahan or e-Challan app?

Do not install an APK from a WhatsApp, SMS or search-ad link that claims you have a pending challan. Open the official mParivahan app from Google Play, whose developer is National Informatics Centre, or check a challan on echallan.parivahan.gov.in and parivahan.gov.in. If you already installed a fake app, disconnect it from the internet, uninstall it, change banking and email passwords from another device, and call 1930.

Look at the stand or machine, not only the code. A loose, fresh or slightly raised sticker over an older printed code is a warning. In your UPI app, read the payee name, and if it does not match the shop, cancel. You never need to scan a QR code to receive money. Treat it as a scam if you did not enter a contest you trust. Staying safe online

How do I create a strong password and remember it?

Use long passphrases of four or more random words, a different password for every important account, and a password manager to remember them. Turn on two factor authentication for email, banking and social media. Change a password immediately if a site you use was breached, and never share passwords over chat or phone.

Read the full guide: Strong Passwords and Password Managers: A Simple Guide for Everyone

What is two factor authentication, and which type is best?

2FA means logging in needs something you know (a password) plus something you have (a phone, app or key) or something you are (a fingerprint). From weakest to strongest: SMS OTP, authenticator app codes, app push prompts with number matching, and passkeys or hardware security keys. Any 2FA is far better than none. Turn it on for email first, then banking, social media and WhatsApp.

What is a passkey, and why are apps switching to it?

A passkey is a login credential stored on your device or in your password manager that uses public key cryptography. You unlock it with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Because it is tied to the real website, a fake site cannot use it, so passkeys resist phishing. The FIDO Alliance's Passkey Index 2026 reports that 36% of successful sign-ins at participating services used a passkey, up from 26% in its 2025 index.

Is public WiFi safe to use?

Public WiFi is fine for browsing news or maps, especially on HTTPS sites, but avoid banking and payments on it when you can and use mobile data instead. Beware of fake hotspots with official sounding names, never install anything a WiFi login page asks for, turn off auto connect, and keep your phone updated. CERT-In advises avoiding public WiFi for sensitive transactions and using a VPN when necessary.

Does a VPN protect me from every online threat?

A VPN creates an encrypted tunnel from your device to the VPN provider's server. It hides your traffic from the local network, like cafe WiFi, and hides your real IP from websites. It does not stop phishing, malware, scam calls, weak passwords or tracking by accounts you are logged into. It also shifts trust to the VPN provider, so choose carefully.

Starting a cybersecurity career

What is cybersecurity, in plain English?

Cybersecurity is the work of protecting computers, networks, apps and data from people who want to steal, change or break them. Defenders try to keep information confidential, accurate and available. You do not need to be a genius coder to start. You need curiosity, basic networking and Linux, and a habit of practising legally on your own lab.

Read the full guide: What Is Cybersecurity? A Plain English Guide for Absolute Beginners

Which cybersecurity roles can I start with in India?

The most common entry points are SOC analyst (monitoring alerts), IT or network support with a security focus, and GRC (governance, risk and compliance). Penetration testing, cloud security, forensics and application security usually come after you have strong basics. Pick one direction for six months, build small projects for it, and keep your fundamentals strong.

Offers change, so always confirm on the official site. Pair every certificate with a small project you can show.

How should I study cybersecurity in 90 days?

Spend days 1 to 30 on foundations (networking, Linux, how the internet works), days 31 to 60 on security concepts and your home lab (attacks, defences, logs, Wireshark, and nmap on your own lab), and days 61 to 90 on a chosen direction plus proof (CTFs, writeups, a portfolio and one beginner certificate). Study one to two hours a day and keep notes.

How do I set up a safe cybersecurity home lab?

Install a free hypervisor such as VirtualBox, create one Linux virtual machine as your workstation, add one intentionally vulnerable target (for example OWASP Juice Shop or a deliberately vulnerable VM), and connect them on an isolated host-only or internal network. Take snapshots before experiments. Never point lab tools at systems you do not own.

What is a CTF, and how do I start?

A CTF is a set of security puzzles where each solution reveals a secret text called a flag. Beginner friendly options include picoCTF, OverTheWire Bandit and the CTF Arena on this site. Start with easy categories like general skills, web and forensics, keep notes, and write a short writeup for every challenge you solve.

What does a SOC analyst do all day?

A SOC analyst monitors security alerts from tools like SIEM and EDR, decides whether each alert is a real threat or a false positive (triage), investigates the real ones, escalates them with clear notes, and documents everything. L1 analysts focus on triage, L2 on deeper investigation, and L3 and engineers on hunting and tuning. Many SOCs run 24x7 in shifts.

Cyber law in India

What are the main cyber laws in India?

The main laws are the Information Technology Act, 2000 (cyber offences such as unauthorised access, identity theft and cheating by personation), the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (the general criminal law that replaced the IPC from 1 July 2024), CERT-In's directions (incident reporting within 6 hours) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 with its 2025 Rules. What is the Information Technology Act, 2000?

The Information Technology Act, 2000 is India's main law for cyber offences and electronic records. It covers issues such as unauthorised access, identity theft and cheating by personation. It is often read together with the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and with CERT-In directions. For a plain walkthrough, and not as legal advice, read the full guide.

What is the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, and how does it relate to cyber crime?

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 is the general criminal law that replaced the Indian Penal Code from 1 July 2024. Many cyber crimes are still charged under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and some conduct can also fall under BNS provisions. The two laws are often used together. When do the DPDP Rules start applying?

The DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025 with an 18 month phased rollout. Phase 1, from 13 November 2025, set up the Data Protection Board. Phase 2, from 13 November 2026, started the Consent Manager registration framework, with the portal dpbi.gov.in. Phase 3, from 13 May 2027, brings most duties for organisations and rights for individuals into force, including notice, consent, security safeguards and breach reporting.

Trends 2026

How do AI voice cloning and deepfake scams work, and how do I protect my family?

AI tools can copy a voice or face from public videos and voice notes. Scammers use this to impersonate family members, bosses or officials and demand urgent money or OTPs. CERT-In's April 2026 advisory tells individuals to verify voice calls, video messages and urgent requests, especially those involving money. The best family defence is simple: hang up, call back on a saved number, and use a family safe word.

What is SIM binding for WhatsApp and Telegram in India?

On 28 November 2025, the Department of Telecommunications issued directions under the Telecom Cyber Security Rules, 2024 (as amended) to app based communication services that use Indian mobile numbers, including WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal and others. Apps must stay continuously linked to the active SIM in the device, and web sessions must log out at least every 6 hours. News reports in October 2026 said enforcement is set for 1 January 2027.

Why do Indian bank websites now end in .bank.in?

RBI's circular of 22 April 2025 told commercial banks and cooperative banks to move their websites to the exclusive .bank.in domain by 31 October 2025. Only eligible banks can register it, through IDRBT as the exclusive registrar. A bank website that does not end in .bank.in deserves suspicion. Always check the full domain carefully, and remember that scams also arrive by calls and apps.

What should I know about ransomware in 2026?

Ransomware usually starts with exploited vulnerabilities, stolen credentials or phishing, then locks files or steals data and demands payment. Verizon's 2026 DBIR, as reported by CyberScoop, found ransomware in 48% of breaches in its dataset, up from 44%, while 69% of victims did not pay. The best protection is patching, MFA, least privilege and tested offline backups.
