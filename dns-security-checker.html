DNS and Email Security Checker

Can someone send email pretending to be your college, company or bank? Type a domain and get an A to F grade for its email and DNS security, with the exact record to add for every problem.

What leaves your browser: only the domain name, sent to Cloudflare's public DNS resolver (cloudflare-dns.com) to read public DNS records. Nothing is sent to CyberWithMadhav and nothing is stored.

What is checked and how it is graded

Each record earns points; the grade is the share of points earned. Email checks weigh the most because spoofed email is the most common way attacks start.

  • SPF (35 points): one valid v=spf1 record, no more than 10 DNS lookups across all includes (RFC 7208), and an ending of ~all or -all.
  • DMARC (35 points): a v=DMARC1 record, policy quarantine or reject, and a rua reporting address.
  • DKIM (10 points): a public key at one of 16 common selectors. Selectors cannot be listed from DNS, so "not found" can mean "not guessed".
  • DNSSEC (5), CAA (5), MTA-STS (3) and TLS-RPT (2). BIMI is shown for information only.
  • Domains with no MX record are graded on whether they clearly say they send no email (v=spf1 -all and DMARC reject), which stops spoofing of unused domains.

Limits

  • HTTP security headers (CSP, HSTS and others) are not graded: browsers block reading another site's headers (CORS), and this site has no server to do it.
  • Results reflect what Cloudflare's resolver sees at that moment. DNS changes can take time to appear.
  • A good grade means spoofing your domain is hard. It does not mean your mailboxes or website are secure.
