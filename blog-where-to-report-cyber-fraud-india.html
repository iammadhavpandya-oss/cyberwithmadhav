Where to Report Cyber Fraud in India

India has several official ways to report fraud, and each one has a different job. Using the right one first can make a real difference, especially when money is involved.

Quick answer

Lost money? Call 1930 first, then your bank, then complete the complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. Only got a suspicious call, SMS or WhatsApp? Report it on Sanchar Saathi Chakshu. Hacked account, fake profile or harassment, no money lost? File on cybercrime.gov.in.

Which one, when?

  • 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline): call immediately when money has been lost to online fraud.
  • cybercrime.gov.in (National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal): file the full complaint, for both financial fraud and other cyber crimes.
  • Chakshu on Sanchar Saathi: report suspicious calls, SMS or WhatsApp messages, even if you lost nothing.
  • Your bank or payment app: block cards or UPI and raise your own complaint about the transaction.

If you lost money

  1. Call 1930. You give your details and transaction details, and get an acknowledgement number by SMS.
  2. Call your bank on the number printed on your card or in its official app. Block what is at risk and note the complaint number.
  3. Complete the complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. The Department of Financial Services says complainants should do this within 24 hours using the acknowledgement number.

Speed matters, because money moves quickly between accounts. Our first hour guide has a minute by minute plan.

If you only got a suspicious call or message

Use Chakshu on Sanchar Saathi. It accepts fraud attempts by call, SMS or WhatsApp, such as fake KYC, electricity or gas disconnection, government impersonation and fake offers. The Sanchar Saathi site clearly says Chakshu is not for reporting money already lost. For that, use 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in. The site also asks you to report within 30 days for action.

Hacked accounts, fake profiles and harassment

For crimes without money loss, such as a hacked social media account, a fake profile using your photos, or online harassment, file on cybercrime.gov.in. Also use the report tools inside the app, and secure your accounts with two factor authentication.

Why your bank still needs its own complaint

The police complaint and the bank complaint are separate. Your bank needs to know quickly so it can block your accounts and look at the transaction. Our reporting guide explains the RBI's customer liability rules and why reporting quickly matters.

Stay safe while reporting

  • Dial 1930 yourself and type cybercrime.gov.in and sancharsaathi.gov.in yourself.
  • Do not use numbers from search ads or social media comments.
  • Real police do not charge to recover money. Anyone asking for a fee is running a second scam.

Key takeaways

  • Money lost: 1930, then bank, then cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Suspicious call or message, no loss: Chakshu.
  • Non money cyber crimes: cybercrime.gov.in.
  • All of these are free. Never pay a "recovery agent".

Official sources

  • Department of Financial Services: Cyber security and fintech (1930, NCRP, Chakshu)
  • Sanchar Saathi: Chakshu, report suspected fraud communication
  • PIB: Chakshu facility on Sanchar Saathi (4 March 2024)
  • cybercrime.gov.in: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Links open the official sites. Procedures and rules can change, so check them before you rely on this post.
