Why Indian Bank Websites Now End in .bank.in, and How It Helps You Spot Fakes

If you visited your bank's website recently, you may have noticed a new address ending in .bank.in. This is not a cosmetic change. It is a simple anti phishing tool that every customer can use.

Quick answer

RBI's circular of 22 April 2025 told commercial banks and cooperative banks to move their websites to the exclusive .bank.in domain by 31 October 2025. Only eligible banks can register it, through IDRBT as the exclusive registrar. So a bank website that does not end in .bank.in deserves suspicion. But always check the full domain carefully, and remember that scams also arrive by calls and apps.

What RBI decided

In its Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies of 7 February 2025, RBI announced exclusive internet domains, bank.in for banks and fin.in for the wider financial sector, to fight fraud in digital payments. Its circular RBI/2025-26/28 of 22 April 2025 operationalised the .bank.in domain through the Institute for Development and Research in Banking Technology (IDRBT), authorised by NIXI under MeitY as the exclusive registrar.

All commercial banks, primary urban cooperative banks, state cooperative banks and district central cooperative banks were advised to complete migration no later than 31 October 2025.

How far has it gone?

Business Today reported on 31 October 2025 that several major banks, including SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank and Punjab National Bank, had moved to .bank.in addresses, with old addresses redirecting. The Economic Times later reported NIXI's CEO saying more than 400 banks had moved, that some missed the deadline, and that all major entities had complied.

Why a special domain helps

Phishing sites copy bank logos and designs perfectly. What they cannot easily copy is a restricted domain. Anyone can register something like sbi-kyc-update.com or hdfc-netbanking-login.in in minutes. But .bank.in can only be registered by eligible banks through IDRBT. So the domain becomes a simple trust signal that does not depend on how real the page looks.

How to check a bank link correctly

Read the domain from right to left, starting from the end of the address before the first single slash:

  • https://www.examplebank.bank.in/login ends in .bank.in. Good sign.
  • https://examplebank.bank.in.secure-login.com/ actually ends in secure-login.com. Fake.
  • https://bank.in-examplebank.com/ ends in in-examplebank.com. Fake.
  • https://examplebank-bank.in/ ends in -bank.in, which is not the same as .bank.in. Suspicious.

The safest habit is still to type your bank's address yourself or use the official app, rather than clicking links in SMS, email or WhatsApp. You can also check a suspicious link with the link checker on this site without opening it.

What .bank.in cannot protect you from

  • Scam calls pretending to be bank staff. A phone call has no domain.
  • Fake apps and APK files sent on WhatsApp.
  • Remote access tricks, where a caller asks you to install screen sharing apps.
  • Lookalike tricks if you do not actually read the domain.
  • Fake customer care numbers found through search. Use the Sanchar Saathi trusted contact details service or your bank's official app.

Teach your family in one line

"Bank ki website ka address .bank.in pe khatam hona chahiye. Warna mat kholo." Then show them the address bar of your bank's real site on their phone, so they know what it looks like. For other common SMS tricks, read our phishing SMS guide.

Frequently asked questions

My bank's old website address still works. Is it fake? Many banks redirect old addresses to the new .bank.in domain. After the page loads, check that the address bar shows a .bank.in domain.

Do bank apps also change? The circular is about website domains. For apps, install only the official app from the Play Store or App Store, checking the developer name.

What about NBFCs, insurance companies and other financial firms? RBI's February 2025 announcement also mentioned a fin.in domain for the wider financial sector. Until that is widely used, check domains carefully and use official apps.

Can a scam SMS contain a real looking .bank.in link? Scammers may try tricks like .bank.in appearing in the middle of a longer address. Always read the end of the domain, and prefer typing the address yourself.

Is a .bank.in website automatically safe to enter my details on? It is a strong signal that the site belongs to a bank. Still, use it only for your own bank, check the full domain and never share OTPs or PINs on a phone call.

Key takeaways

  • Indian bank websites moved to the exclusive .bank.in domain under an RBI circular.
  • Only eligible banks can register .bank.in, through IDRBT.
  • Read the domain right to left: it should end exactly in .bank.in.
  • The domain helps with fake websites, not with scam calls or fake apps.
