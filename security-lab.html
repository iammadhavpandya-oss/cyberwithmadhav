Email and Link Security Lab

Three analyst tools for the way most attacks in India start: an email or a link. Grade any domain's email security, take apart the headers of a suspicious email, and dissect a phishing URL without ever opening it.

Privacy first. Header and URL analysis run fully offline in your browser. The DNS checker sends only the domain name you type to Cloudflare's public DNS resolver. No accounts, no cookies, nothing stored on our side.

DNS and Email Security Checker

An A to F grade for any domain, with the exact record to fix each problem.

  • SPF with recursive lookup count (limit 10)
  • DMARC policy, pct and reporting
  • DKIM at common selectors
  • DNSSEC, CAA, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, BIMI

Peel a suspicious link layer by layer. It is never opened or expanded.

  • Punycode and homoglyph detection
  • Typosquats of Indian banks, UPI and gov.in
  • Redirect wrappers and hidden destinations
  • @ tricks, IP hosts, APK downloads

Who it is for

Students and freshers learning blue team and SOC skills: these are the same checks an analyst runs during phishing triage. IT teams at colleges and schools: see in one minute whether anyone can send email as your principal or your exam cell. Everyone else: get a second opinion on a scary email or link before you click.

Method and honest limits

  • Results come from public DNS data and from the text you paste. The tools do not open links, log in anywhere or scan servers.
  • A browser cannot read another website's HTTP security headers (CORS blocks it), so there is no header grade here. Use a server-side scanner for that.
  • Official domain lists cover common Indian banks, payment apps, government portals and big brands. A domain missing from the list is not automatically fake.
  • No tool can prove a message is safe. When money or passwords are involved, contact the organisation through its official app or a number you already have.
