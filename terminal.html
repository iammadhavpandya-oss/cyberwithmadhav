Educational simulation: nothing runs on a real system. Practise only on machines you own or are authorised to use. See Terms.

    How it works
    
      
      
        
    • Find the password. Every level hides a 32 character password somewhere in its files. Paste it below the terminal to unlock the next level.
      • 
        
    • Use real commands. The shell supports pipes, quotes, backslash escapes, * and ? wildcards, 2>/dev/null and Linux style permissions.
      • 
        
    • Hints cost points. Each level is worth 100 points. Opening the hint before you solve it costs 25.
      • 
        
    • Saved on this device. Progress lives in your browser storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
