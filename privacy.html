01Summary

CyberWithMadhav is a static website. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, we do not set cookies, and you do not need an account to use the site.

A few settings are kept in your own browser so the site remembers your choices. They never leave your device unless you use an optional feature described below.

02Who we are

The site is run by Madhav Pandya under the name CyberWithMadhav. You can reach us at madhavpandya@zoho.in.

03What is stored in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage and session storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Browser storage used by the site
KeyWhereWhat it holds
cwm-consentLocal storageThat you accepted the Terms and Privacy Policy, the version you accepted and when.
cwm-themeLocal storageYour light or dark theme choice.
cwm-ctf-practice-v2, cwm-ctf-prefs, cwm-ctf-ui, cwm-ctf-quotesLocal storageYour CTF practice progress and best scores, your CTF settings (such as sound and motion), and which quotes you have already seen.
cwm-ctf-sessionLocal storageOnly if a CTF leaderboard backend is connected and you create a nickname: your nickname and sign in token.
cwm-prompt-favsLocal storageThe prompts you marked as favourites.
cwm-offlineCache storageCopies of the site's own pages, styles, scripts and data, kept by the service worker so pages load faster and work offline. Nothing you type is cached.
cwm-varsLocal storageLab values you type into the cheat sheets, such as a target IP for your own lab.
cwm-banner, cwm-stars:*Session storageWhether you closed the alert banner, and a short cache of public GitHub star counts. Cleared when you close the tab.

You can remove all of it at any time by clearing site data for this site in your browser settings. If you clear the consent record, you will see the welcome screen again.

04Services the site relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts are self-hosted on this site (Inter, Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono). Your browser loads them from our own assets, not from a third-party font CDN.
  • GitHub API: on the home page, when you scroll near the About section, your browser may fetch public star counts for the listed projects from api.github.com.

We do not receive any personal information from these services.

05Contact and optional leaderboard

The site has no newsletter. The contact form on the home page does not send anything to a server: it opens your own email app with your message filled in, and you decide whether to send it. If you email us, we receive what you write and your email address, and use them only to reply to you.

The CTF leaderboard works only when an optional backend is connected. At the time of writing, no backend is connected on the live site, so CTF progress stays in your browser.

If a leaderboard backend is connected in future, it will store only your chosen nickname and scores, along with basic technical data such as a hashed IP address used to prevent abuse. It will be used only to run the leaderboard, and never sold. We will update this policy before that happens.

06Your choices and rights

You can use the site without giving us any personal information. You can clear browser storage at any time. If you have sent us information by email or a form, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it by writing to madhavpandya@zoho.in.

07Children

The site is an educational resource for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and teachers are welcome to use the awareness content with children.

08Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and ask you to accept the updated version on your next visit. This policy forms part of our Terms and Conditions.
