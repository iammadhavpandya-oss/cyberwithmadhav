    How scoring works
    • Each level has about 5 or 6 questions: 10 marks each at Beginner, 20 at Intermediate, 30 at Expert.
    • Multiple choice: one attempt. Ordering questions: 3 attempts. Typed answers and flags: up to 5 attempts, and from the 3rd attempt each try costs 2 marks.
    • A hint costs 25% of that question's marks (a second hint, where there is one, takes it to 50%).
    • Timers are soft (45, 75 and 120 seconds). Running over never costs marks; finishing early adds up to 20% bonus XP. Relaxed mode turns timers off.
    • Correct answers in a row build a streak multiplier on XP: x1.1 per answer, up to x1.5.
    • XP decides your rank: Recruit, Packet Scout, Shell Walker, Analyst, Operator, Root.
    • Flags look like CWM{something}. Typing just the inside also works.
    • Keys: 1 to 4 pick an option, Enter submits, Esc closes panels.
