For colleges and universities

A guest lecture or cyber security workshop for college students works best when it talks about the scams they actually see: UPI collect requests, fake internship or job offers, phishing on campus Wi-Fi, account takeover and digital arrest calls. I keep the language plain, and I leave time for questions.

The usual length is 60 to 90 minutes. Online sessions are open to colleges anywhere in India. In-person sessions are for Mumbai campuses only. Student councils, NSS units and faculty can email to book. This is an awareness session, not a placement workshop and not legal advice.

For schools

A cyber safety seminar for school students stays age-appropriate. Typical topics are passwords and PINs, what not to share, fake game or study apps, phishing SMS, and when to tell a teacher or parent. A teacher or school staff member must be present throughout.

I do not collect personal data from students. Any hands-on demo runs only on my own test devices. You can also ask for a short teacher or admin briefing, and a one-page handout with 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in. Online across India, or in person at schools in Mumbai.

For clubs and NGOs

Rotary and Lions chapters, NSS units, student clubs, housing societies and NGOs often want a practical talk that families can take home. I cover common Indian scams, QR codes at shops and gates, WhatsApp group safety, and where to report, including 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in.

Sessions can be in English, Hindi, Hinglish or Marathi. Online for clubs anywhere in India, or in person if your venue is in Mumbai. Tell me who will be in the room: students, residents, seniors, a women group, or a mixed audience.

For coaching institutes and corporates

Coaching and IT institutes can book a longer session for batches preparing for jobs or a first role in tech. Along with the college topics, we can cover checking an app before you install it, internships that ask for fees, password managers, and safe habits for a first tech job.

For company staff the usual focus is work email phishing, passwords and 2FA, QR and UPI risks, vendor and attachment caution, how to report, and a short, plain note on the DPDP Act. This is awareness only, not legal advice and not a full compliance audit. Online across India, or in person at Mumbai offices.
