      ⚖️ Ethics & legal
      
        
      Everything here is for learning and defence. Only test systems you own or have explicit written authorisation to test. Unauthorised access, data theft or damage to computer systems is an offence in India under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (e.g. Sections 43 and 66) and can lead to fines and imprisonment. Use labs like TryHackMe, Hack The Box and PortSwigger Academy to practise legally.
