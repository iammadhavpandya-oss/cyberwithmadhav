Who books an in-person session in Mumbai

This page is for people in Mumbai who want a speaker on site: colleges and schools, coaching institutes, company offices, housing societies, NGOs, and clubs such as Rotary, Lions, NSS or a student club. I come to your venue. There is no separate office address to visit.

What an offline Mumbai session can cover

The topics follow the audience in the room. A college or corporate group often needs UPI fraud, phishing, fake job offers or work email caution. A housing society, Rotary or Lions meeting often needs a family-friendly briefing: OTP scams, QR codes at shops and gates, WhatsApp safety, and where to report. A women group can also cover account safety, harassment reporting and loan app caution. I confirm the outline with you before the date.

What to arrange at the venue

Please share the full Mumbai address, a contact person on the day, and the room setup. A projector or screen, a microphone if the room is large, and a stable internet link help the session run smoothly. If a facility is missing, tell me in the email and we will adapt. Sessions can be in English, Hindi, Hinglish or Marathi.

If you are not in Mumbai, you can still book. Online sessions are open to institutes, clubs and companies anywhere in India, and abroad if the time works.

Questions organisers in Mumbai usually ask

Which parts of Mumbai do you come to for an offline session?

I come to your venue within Mumbai. There is no separate office address to visit. Please share the full venue address, the room setup, and facilities such as a projector, a microphone and internet. If your venue is outside Mumbai, book an online session instead.

Can Rotary, Lions, a company or a housing society book you as a speaker?

Yes. Rotary and Lions chapters, companies, housing societies, NGOs, women groups and other community groups can email to book. Tell me who will be in the room and roughly how many people will attend. Online bookings are also open if an in-person date in Mumbai does not work.

Which languages can a Mumbai session be in?

English, Hindi, Hinglish or Marathi. Pick the language your audience is most comfortable with, or a mix if that helps.
