Who is Madhav Pandya?

Madhav Pandya is a cybersecurity content creator based in Mumbai, India, and the founder of CyberWithMadhav. He explains online safety, scams and Indian cyber law to beginners and families, in plain English and Hinglish, without jargon and without fear tactics.

He is studying for a BA LLB at KES Shri J.P. Law College and a BCA with a specialisation in Cloud Computing and Cyber Security. He also runs cyber awareness sessions for colleges and other groups. You can read the long version on the About page.

What I create

Everything is made for people in India and keeps to official sources, with links, so you can check it yourself.

  • Starting a cybersecurity career in India, with a free step by step learning path.
